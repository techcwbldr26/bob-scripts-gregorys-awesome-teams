## Latest News

[STATE AUDITORS MEET IN TACOMA FOR 48TH ANNUAL CONFERENCE](https://www.nasact.org/article_content.asp?edition=3&section=2&article=233)

NSAA President Pat McCarthy recently welcomed state auditors to Tacoma, Washington, for the forty-eighth annual conference of the National State Auditors Association. The conference was held June 10-12 and was attended by approximately 230 individuals, including 28 principal state auditors and representation from 36 states plus Puerto Rico and the District of Columbia.

[Learn More](https://www.nasact.org/article_content.asp?edition=3&section=2&article=233)

[AN INVITATION FROM NASACT PRESIDENT STEVE MCCOY](https://www.nasact.org/article_content.asp?edition=3&section=1&article=232)

The annual conference will once again bring together state treasurers, auditors, and comptrollers along with loyal sponsors to learn, exchange ideas, and strengthen our profession we care deeply about. As state governments navigate increasingly complex challenges, the opportunity to connect with peers and industry leaders has never been more important.

[Learn More](https://www.nasact.org/article_content.asp?edition=3&section=1&article=232)

[OMB PROPOSES CHANGES TO UNIFORM GUIDANCE](https://www.nasact.org/article_content.asp?edition=3&section=8&article=231)

The proposal includes changes that could affect all NASACT member offices. We strongly encourage you to review the proposal and provide your comments and observations. We also ask that you provide a copy of your response to NASACT so that we may craft an association-wide response.

Comments are due to NASACT by July 1, 2026, and to OMB by July 13, 2026. The turnaround is short because this proposal has a 45-day comment period.

[Learn More](https://www.nasact.org/article_content.asp?edition=3&section=8&article=231)
