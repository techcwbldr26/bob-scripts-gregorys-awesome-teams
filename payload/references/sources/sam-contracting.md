# Contracting

SAM.gov is a centralized source for finding and bidding on U.S. government contract opportunities, awards and publishing subcontract reports.

**Download Contract Data.** Visit the [Data Bank](https://sam.gov/reports/awards/standard) to download or run reports on contracting data.

**Connect to our Contract Data APIs.** Visit [Open.gsa(link is external)](https://open.gsa.gov/api/ "(opens in a new window)") to connect through APIs to the contracting data.

**Search Contracting Data** Select "Contracting" from the search box below. Enter the search criteria. Refine the search by selecting a specific domain ("Contract Awards "Contract Awards" "Subcontract Reports").
