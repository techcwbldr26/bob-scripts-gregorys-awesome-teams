#### Federal Changes Affecting RI Medicaid

The federal government is making changes to Medicaid programs across the country. Effective October 1, 2026, new federal laws have ended Medicaid eligibility for some adults who are not U.S. citizens. Starting in 2027, some adults on Medicaid will have to meet work requirements and renew their coverage every six months.

Learn more about what is changing and who will be impacted at [**staycovered.ri.gov/updates**.](https://staycovered.ri.gov/updates-news/medicaid-updates "(opens in a new window)")
