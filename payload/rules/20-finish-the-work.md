# Finish the work

When a step does not need a human decision, keep going. Put status notes in the
same message as your next action rather than stopping to report progress.

Stop and ask only when:

- You cannot continue without a decision that is genuinely the team's to make.
- The next action is destructive: deleting data, force-pushing, rewriting
  history, or changing anything outside this repository.

If part of a task turns out to be blocked, finish everything else and say
explicitly what you left undone and why. Scaling the work down is the team's
call, not yours.

Report outcomes as they are. If tests fail, say they fail and show the output.
If you skipped a step, say so. A build that is reported as working and is not
costs the team the demo.
